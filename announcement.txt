The Aura Microwave Limb Sounder (MLS) team is pleased to announce the release of a new "version 5" MLS dataset.  The largest changes between version 5 (v5) and earlier versions are in the water vapor (H2O) and Nitrous Oxide (N2O) products.  Version 5 has ameliorated a well-documented "dry bias" in the water vapor product in the altitudes 2-3 km below the tropopause.  Additionally, a slow drift in H2O starting in 2010 has been at least partly ameliorated, as has a related decreasing drift in the N2O product.  The only other MLS standard products affected by this drift are Hydrogen Cyanide (HCN) and upper stratospheric nitric acid (HNO3).  Remaining MLS products including ozone (O3), HCl, and HNO3 in the lower stratosphere, are not impacted.  More minor changes to other products include extension of the H2O, ozone, temperature, and Carbon Monoxide (CO) products to higher altitudes in the mesosphere/thermosphere.

Reprocessing of the entire 15+ year MLS record with the v5 algorithms is expected to complete in mid-to-late 2021.  Until that reprocessing is complete, incoming MLS data will continue to be processed using older v4 algorithms as well as v5, in order to facilitate continued scientific analyses using a continuous dataset.

A preliminary assessment of the v5 data quality, along with guidelines for appropriate use of the MLS v5 data, is given in the data quality document (https://mls.jpl.nasa.gov/data/v4-2_data_quality_document.pdf).  Updates to this document are planned in the coming months, when more v5 data will be available for characterization and re-validation, and when updated estimates of systematic uncertainty are completed.
