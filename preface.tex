\chapter*{Preface}

One should perhaps be skeptical whenever it is stated from the outset that a large written work is intended to be a ``living document''.  Such predictions seem to be borne out only rarely, and the resulting documents grow stale sooner than hoped.  That said, over the last two decades, circumstances have conspired to make this document an example of one such long-lasting and evolving document.  Previously, whenever a new version of the MLS dataset was generated, it has been accompanied by an updated version of this document.  Those updates were written with the recognition that there continues to be scope to improve the MLS data record, and that the team had plans and the resources to do so.  However, this iteration is different, as it appears that \thisv may well be the final version of MLS data of record, and thus this the final version of this document. 

At the time of writing the future for the Aura mission in general, and MLS observations in particular, is unclear.  The Aura mission is set to be terminated in 2026.  Some MLS team members have retired, so previous mechanisms for contacting them are no longer active.  In the coming months and years, this will probably be the case for other team members.  Accordingly, statements made in earlier versions of this document that data users should ``contact the MLS team with any questions'' are perhaps less helpful than they were before.  The team intends to respond to queries sent to \href{mailto:mls-data@jpl.nasa.gov}{\texttt{mls-data@jpl.nasa.gov}} for as long as feasible, ideally beyond the closeout of the MLS project.  As an aid to contacting team members at a later date the table below \mycomment{Table needed} lists the \href{https://orcid.org/}{ORCIDs} for authors of this document. That said, if this becomes the only means for contacting individuals, it should be understood that any assistance given at that juncture would likely be on an ``as time allows'' basis.

Having been proposed in 1988, launched in 2004, and continuing to operate at the time of writing, Aura MLS represents nearly four decades of effort by countless talented and dedicated individuals.  It has been a privilege for those of us continuing to work on the project to reap the rewards of their efforts and serve as stewards of this unique dataset that has resulted.  It has also been a privilege and a true pleasure to witness the discoveries that the MLS observations have provided.  We dedicate this document to all those involved in the MLS project in any way: people who developed the ground-breaking technologies that enabled the technique, those who had the vision and drive to propose Aura MLS for flight, people who designed, built, tested and calibrated the instrument, those working on the Aura mission and spacecraft, people operating the instrument and spacecraft, those working on data processing algorithms and ground systems, the many contributors to data validation, and, finally, to you --- the scientists and others continuing to use MLS data to advance our understanding of the Earth system.  Our profound thanks!

\null\hfill The MLS team.
